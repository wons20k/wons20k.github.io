About

I am a Ph.D. student at the POSTECH Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Tae-Hyun Oh. Before that, I received my B.Sc. degree from the University of Birmingham, where I was advised by Prof. Hyung Jin Chang and Prof. Jianbo Jiao.

My research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision and robotics, with a particular emphasis on:

  • Vision-Language Models (Perception, reasoning, and evaluation)
  • Embodied AI (Grounding multimodal understanding into physical actions)
